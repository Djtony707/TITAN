TITAN Mascot Concepts

Warm, desk-bound companions — each hand-crafted for the wood surface

Owl Scholar
Classic · Wise
A brass spectacled owl perched atop a leather-bound book. Represents knowledge, deliberation, and the keeper of the desk. Its presence says "this workspace has depth."
Brass Automaton
Steampunk · Mechanical
A pocket-sized clockwork bird assembled from gears and polished brass. It ticks softly and occasionally whirs its gears when TITAN is thinking. Industrial warmth without the cold of chrome.
Fox Quill
Clever · Agile
A russet fox with a brass quill tucked behind its ear. Quick-witted and always ready to write the next thought. The quill tip leaves faint ink trails when excited.
Parchment Wolf
Explorer · Map-maker
A wolf formed from folded parchment and ink lines, wearing a tiny compass. When a mission reaches new territory, the map-lines on its fur redraw themselves. Stoic and dependable.
Desk Dragon
Guardian · Magical
A small brass-horned dragon curled around an inkwell no bigger than a thimble. Its wings fold into desk ornaments when idle. Warmly protective, slightly mischievous.
Lantern Spirit
Ethereal · Gentle
A soft amber spirit carrying a tiny brass lantern. It drifts slowly above the desk and glows brighter when TITAN has something important to share. Not a robot — a presence.
Clockwork Cat
Curious · Watchful
A wooden mechanical cat with brass eyes and a spring-wind tail. It perches on paper stacks and tracks the cursor. Tail flicks when new data arrives. Familiar and comforting.
Tome Beetle
Scavenger · Archivist
A beetle whose brass-gilded wing-cases look like miniature book covers. It burrows through stacks of papers and emerges with the right document. Industrious, quiet, essential.
